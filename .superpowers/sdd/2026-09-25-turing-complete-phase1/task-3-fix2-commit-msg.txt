fix(core): price the built-in 1-bit gates in NAND equivalents

Fix round 2 of the Task 3 review: the gate metric was still two different
units. `gateCost()` falls back to `cost`, and the phase-0 `gate()` factory
stated only `cost: 1`, so a built-in `and` was worth 1 while one bit of `and8`
was worth 2 -- both stamped "NAND equivalent".

* `defs/index.ts`: every 1-bit gate now states `gateCost` on the basis
  `wide.ts` documents -- NAND 1, NOT 1, AND 2, OR 3, NOR 4, XOR 4, XNOR 5,
  AND3 4, OR3 6 -- with each number's all-NAND construction in a comment and
  in one table. `cost` stays 1 on all nine gates, so the DELAY metric is
  unchanged.
* The now-false doc claims are corrected (`defs/index.ts`, `registry.ts`,
  `grader.ts`, plus `wide.ts`'s "Exact for the widths..." line): `cost` is the
  per-node delay unit, `gateCost` the NAND-equivalent count, the two coincide
  only for `nand`/`not`, and the `?? cost` fallback exists for the defs whose
  two values genuinely agree because both are zero (rails, wiring, storage).
* `ashr8`'s sign fill is now documented as a deliberate exclusion from the
  "amount >= 8 -> 0" rule (ratified; behaviour unchanged).
* Recalibrated the three chapter-1 targets their own reference solutions no
  longer met: ch1-06 gate 2 -> 4, ch1-10 gate 2 -> 6, ch1-11 gate 2 -> 4
  (measured, not guessed). No delay or tick metric or target moved.
* New tests: a shipped-set check that every level's reference solution meets
  every one of its own `threeStar` bounds (and has a reference circuit at all);
  explicit phase-0 gate counts in `registry.test.ts`; and a cross-check in
  `defs-wide.test.ts` that each bit-sliced wide cell costs its width times the
  registered 1-bit gate -- so the two tables cannot drift into two bases.

Verified: full suite 350 passed / 16 files, `tsc --noEmit` 0 diagnostics,
`pnpm build` exit 0, `pnpm smoke` 2 passed. Mutation checks: removing a
built-in gate's `gateCost` fails the freeze, the explicit-count and the
cross-basis tests; reverting ch1-11's target to 2 fails the new shipped-set
check while the freeze stays green.
