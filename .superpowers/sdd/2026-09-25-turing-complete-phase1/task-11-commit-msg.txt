feat(levels): chapter 2 batch 4 -- levels 28-38, storage and timing

The storage and timing half of chapter 2, and the first chapter-2 batch the
`script` checker carries: the loop latch (28), the delayed byte (29), the clock
source (30), the conditional inverter and switch (31, 32), the byte selector
and the reframed bus (33, 34), the conditional writes (35, 36), the 256-byte
box (37) and the counter (38).

- every level carries a SOURCED/AUTHORED data comment; the source fixes names,
  order and one concept line only, so ports, widths, checks, targets and
  rewards are this replica's design
- spec 3.1 forbids a bus protocol (multi-driver arbitration), so level 34 is
  reframed as driver selection and says so in both its brief and its comment
- level 30 asserts periodicity over sixteen alternating ticks plus a hold while
  disabled, and a second script holds a disabled source low from reset
- level 37's check writes all 256 addresses and reads all 256 back with load
  low and the complement of the stored byte on `d`
- level 38 records the source's "achievement: <= 65 gates" as an achievement
  rather than a pass condition, and withholds `counter8`, the drop-in
- threeStar targets are each reference solution's own measured metrics
- the script levels are authored to the checker's real tick semantics: a step's
  inputs are written and settled BEFORE its tick advances, so holding is proved
  by a second step at the same tick (levels 28, 29, 35, 36, 38)
