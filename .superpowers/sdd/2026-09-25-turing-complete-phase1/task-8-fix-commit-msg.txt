fix(progress): offer a level its own rewards, and unlock mem1 from ch1-12

Two playability defects, and the whole-set check that would have caught both.

`paletteDefsFor` now folds the level's OWN rewards into the unlocked set
before filtering by `allowedComponents`. The parts a level hands out are
the parts its puzzle was designed around, so withholding them until the
player has solved it is backwards: level 13's parity puzzle cannot be built
without the `splitter` it rewards, because every chapter-1 part has 1-bit
pins and a wire from the 4-bit input copies bit 0 alone. The asymmetry is
deliberate and documented at the function: the own reward is offered to
BUILD with, while the palette stays bounded by the level's own list, so a
part the level does not offer stays out even once unlocked. `unlockedComponents`
is untouched -- a save must not gain parts from a level the player has not
passed.

This is a no-op for chapter 1, measured rather than assumed: no chapter-1
level lists its own reward, and every chapter-1 reference draws only on
parts an earlier level rewards. Dumping all 17 shipped palettes under the
old rule and the new one leaves the twelve chapter-1 lines byte-identical;
the four chapter-2 lines that change gain exactly their own listed rewards
(13: splitter/maker/const8, 15: less_u, 16: equal8, 17: add8/mul8).

`ch1-12-binary-racer` rewards `mem1`: chapter 1's last two levels rewarded
nothing, and `mem1` was defined in `core/defs/index.ts` and rewarded by no
level anywhere, so chapter 2's latch level had no latch part it could ever
offer. The capstone is the natural first home for "hold a bit". Not added
to `STARTER_COMPONENTS`, which would put it in level 1's palette. Rewards
are not scored (`grade()` reads the checks and the `threeStar` metrics
only), chapter 1's tests run unchanged and in the same numbers, and level
12's own palette is unchanged because it does not list `mem1`.

New `test/levels/level-buildability.test.ts` (40 tests) walks every shipped
level -- `LEVELS`/`LEVEL_ORDER`, never a hardcoded list -- and calls
`paletteDefsFor` instead of re-deriving it:

* every part in the level's reference solution is in the palette the app
  builds for a player who has passed every PRIOR level and not this one;
* every name in `allowedComponents` is a starter or rewarded by a level at
  or before it, and nothing outside that list reaches the palette.

Written first: 3 failed / 37 passed, naming level 13's missing splitter and
`mem1` as unlocked by no shipped level. Chapter 2 is not joined into
`LEVEL_ORDER` yet, so the walk covers chapter 1 plus the shipped chapter-2
batch (17 ids) and picks up every later batch as it joins `CH2_LEVELS` --
which turns "level 28 has no latch part" into a failure the moment level 28
lists it.
